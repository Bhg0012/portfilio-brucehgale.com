## Facts used on the site

- Founded May 2026; one-person agency; Bruce handles sales, design, SEO, ads, billing, and client support.
- Serves trade contractors (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers, roofers, painters) in Alabama, mainly Huntsville and Auburn.
- Offer: no upfront cost, flat monthly subscription covering design, hosting, SSL, maintenance, minor updates, domain management, core local SEO, and Google Business Profile setup. Billing through Stripe.
- 14 active subscription clients (Bruce's expenses sheet; snapshot date not stated).
- Client acquisition: cold calls with mockups and a Make.com scraper into a Google Sheet (earlier), then Meta lead ads with Instant Forms feeding GoHighLevel with automatic follow-up.
- Build process: GoHighLevel templates, its AI builder drafts content, Bruce customizes; Stripe billing; domain, hosting, SSL; local SEO; Loom walkthrough at handoff; requests tracked as GHL tasks.
- SEO work: metadata, keyword research, schema markup, indexing and robots.txt troubleshooting.
- Website-offer Meta ads paused by Oct 11, 2026, because clients struggled to see results from a site alone and some churned.
- Pivot: automated Google review request system (texts customers, weekly follow-ups for four weeks), in development.
- AI used in the work: GoHighLevel AI website builder (first drafts), Claude (strategy, copy, SEO audits), Gemini and Veo (ad video and image content). No AI features recorded on client websites.
- Lessons recorded in Bruce's notes: a website alone is hard to judge as working, which drives churn; work existing leads and upsells before more ad spend; prove one market before adding another.

## Summary

Four-person audit of Auburn University's Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, and TikTok over Nov 2024 to Nov 2025, with three audience personas, a four-school competitive analysis, and platform-specific recommendations in a 45-page report.

## Facts (with source IDs)

- Problem: evaluate how effectively Auburn uses each platform to support marketing and communication goals, and compare with peers (S004).
- Audience: Auburn's social media team (S002 says Bruce worked directly with them).
- Bruce's contributions (his own words, S002): analyzed performance to identify KPIs, audience insights, and platform-specific growth strategies; worked directly with Auburn's social media team to translate analytics into recommendations.
- Team: Bruce Gale, Carson Faloni, Georgia Kate Scott, Mary Allen Richardson (S004).
- Approach: personas, platform analysis against 2025 Hootsuite benchmarks, competitive review, recommendations with "why it matters" and expected outcomes (S004).
- Key findings: Instagram #1 in impressions (31.9M); Facebook #1 in engagements (1.48M); LinkedIn and TikTok lead engagement rate (11.43%, 11.3%); TikTok posts only 3 to 5 times a month (S004).
- Tools: not stated. Metric panels resemble Sprout Social (unconfirmed). Report in Google Slides.
- AI: none stated.
- Dates: Nov 2024 to Nov 2025 per the cover (S004).

## Why it mattered

For a university, social media is a front door. Prospective students use it to picture campus life, current students use it to stay connected, and alumni use it to stay involved and give back. Auburn posts across five major platforms, and each one reaches a different audience. The question was whether each platform was doing its job.

## How we approached it

- **Start with the audience.** We wrote three personas (a current student, a prospective student, and an alum) describing which platforms each one uses and what they look for, so the analysis could ask whether each platform serves its real audience.
- **Measure every platform the same way.** For a full year (November 2024 to November 2025) we compared followers, impressions, engagements, and engagement rate on each platform, and checked each rate against the 2025 Hootsuite industry benchmarks.
- **Look at the competition.** We reviewed how two rivals (Alabama and Georgia) and two aspirant schools (Texas A&M and South Carolina) run their strongest channels, and what Auburn could learn from them.

## What the data showed

### Instagram drives reach, Facebook drives engagement

Instagram generated the most impressions of any platform (31.9 million), while Facebook drew the most total engagements (1.48 million) from an audience made up largely of parents and alumni.

### LinkedIn and TikTok punch above their size

The two smaller channels had the highest engagement rates: LinkedIn at 11.43% and TikTok at 11.3%. Their audiences respond strongly to the content they see.

### TikTok was under-posting

Despite that engagement, Auburn's TikTok posted only 3 to 5 times a month, which limits how often the algorithm can surface its videos.

## Recommendations

- **TikTok:** post 2 to 3 times a week to give content that already works more chances to be seen.
- **Facebook:** lean into what its older audience values (tradition, community pride, faculty milestones, nostalgia) and review regularly which posts resonate and which draw pushback.
- **X:** shift from mostly athletics toward original academic and campus stories, with a more personable voice, as South Carolina does well.
- **LinkedIn:** build on its strength with alumni spotlights, career-path stories, and mentorship opportunities.
- **Instagram:** keep an already strong visual brand consistent by giving partner departments shared templates, and use more interactive Stories features like polls and questions.

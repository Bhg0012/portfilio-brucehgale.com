## The problem

When an Alabama homeowner needs a contractor, they search on Google. A trade business that doesn't show up at that moment loses the job to one that does. But the usual route to a website, paying an agency thousands of dollars up front, priced out many small contractors, and the sites they did buy often brought in no customers.

## The model

I built AuburnSEO around a simpler offer:

- **No upfront cost.** Clients pay one flat monthly rate instead of a large design fee.
- **Everything handled.** The plan covers design, hosting, security, domain management, small content updates, core local SEO, and Google Business Profile setup.
- **Predictable revenue.** Every client is a monthly subscription, billed automatically.

## Finding clients

I started with cold calls to contractors who had no website, showing each one a mockup of what their site could look like. To find prospects, I built a small pipeline in Make.com that pulled local business details into a spreadsheet.

Later I moved to Meta lead ads. Someone who sees an ad fills out a short form on Facebook or Instagram, the lead lands in my CRM automatically, and an instant follow-up goes out while I'm busy with client work.

## How a site gets built

1. A new lead arrives from an ad form and gets an automatic follow-up.
2. I gather the business details: services, service area, and contact information.
3. I start from one of a few templates, let GoHighLevel's AI builder draft the content, then edit the copy and design for that business.
4. The client signs up for the monthly plan, with billing set up in Stripe.
5. I connect the domain, hosting, and SSL, and set up local SEO.
6. I send a recorded walkthrough of the finished site, and handle later change requests as tasks in the CRM.

## What the data told me

A website is hard for a contractor to judge as "working." Clients struggled to see results from a site alone, and some cancelled, so by October 2026 I paused the ads for the website offer.

The next offer is designed around a result clients can see: an automated system that texts their customers to ask for Google reviews and follows up weekly until a review is left. Clients can watch their review count climb. It is in development now.

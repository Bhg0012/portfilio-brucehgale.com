## The problem

When an Alabama homeowner needs a contractor, they search on Google. A trade business that doesn't show up at that moment loses the job to one that does. But the usual route to a website, paying an agency thousands of dollars up front, priced out many small contractors, and the sites they did buy often brought in no customers.

## The model

I built AuburnSEO around a simpler offer:

- **No upfront cost.** Clients pay one flat monthly rate instead of a large design fee.
- **Everything handled.** The plan covers design, hosting, security, domain management, small content updates, and core local SEO.
- **Predictable revenue.** Every client is a monthly subscription, billed automatically.

## Finding clients

Almost all of my clients and leads come from Meta ads. Someone who sees an ad fills out a short form on Facebook or Instagram, the lead lands in my GoHighLevel CRM automatically, and an automated follow-up reaches them right away, even while I'm busy building sites. That system lets one person keep a steady pipeline without spending the day on the phone.

## How a site gets built

1. A new lead arrives from an ad form and gets an automatic follow-up.
2. I gather the business details: services, service area, and contact information.
3. I start from one of a few templates, let GoHighLevel's AI builder draft the content, then edit the copy and design for that business.
4. The client signs up for the monthly plan, with billing set up in Stripe.
5. I connect the domain, hosting, and SSL, and set up local SEO.
6. I send a recorded walkthrough of the finished site, and handle later change requests as tasks in the CRM.

## What's next

I'm building an automated review-request system for clients: it texts their customers to ask for a Google review and follows up weekly for up to four weeks or until one is left, so clients can watch their review count grow.

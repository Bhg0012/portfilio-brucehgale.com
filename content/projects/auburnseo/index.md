## The problem

When an Alabama homeowner needs a contractor, they search on Google. A trade business that doesn't show up at that moment loses the job to a competitor that does. But the usual way to get a website, paying an agency thousands of dollars up front, often left contractors with a site that brought in no customers at all.

## The model

I built AuburnSEO around a simpler offer:

- **No upfront cost.** Clients pay one flat monthly rate, and billing starts only when their site goes live.
- **Cancel anytime.** Clients are not locked into a long contract.
- **Everything included.** Each plan covers the design, build, and hosting of a mobile-first website, the domain, and ongoing local SEO.

## What I build

- **Websites designed to win jobs.** Mobile-first sites built for lead generation in each client's service area, with clear buttons to call or book.
- **Google Business Profiles.** I set up and manage each client's Google profile, which drives local search and map results.
- **Local marketing.** Advertising and search optimization aimed at the homeowners in each client's area.

AuburnSEO focuses on the trades: construction, cabinetry, repair services, and landscaping.

## Running the business

Beyond the client work, I set up the systems behind the agency: the lead-generation site at auburnseo.com, booking and lead forms (including Meta ad lead forms), text and email follow-up, and recurring billing through Stripe.

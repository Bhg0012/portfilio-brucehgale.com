## The business

I launched a print-on-demand T-shirt shop on Etsy: each shirt is printed and shipped only after it is ordered, so there is no inventory to buy up front. I ran the operation end to end, including the marketing and the numbers behind it.

## Focusing on campus

I aimed my advertising at the Auburn campus, and the customer data shows how concentrated the business became. In 2023, 59% of customers were in Alabama. In 2024, 88% were, and Auburn alone made up 95% of Alabama customers, up from 38%.

## Using my own data

- **Dashboards and charts:** pivot tables, charts, and heat maps to track sales and customers.
- **Customer map:** a geographic view of customer locations, which made the concentration in Alabama and the Southeast obvious.
- **Delivery prediction:** a classification model that predicts whether an order will take more or less than one week to arrive, based on shipping distance and whether the order falls on a weekend or holiday.
